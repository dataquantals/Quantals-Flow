FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan never asks for payment details, and trials can start without one.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time from your billing settings.

What happens to my boards if I downgrade?

Your boards are never deleted. On Free you keep 3 editable boards and the rest become read-only.

How does per-user pricing work?

You're billed per active user in your team each month, with annual billing saving roughly 20%.

Is there a discount for non-profits?

Yes. Registered non-profits, students and educators can apply for discounted or free access.
